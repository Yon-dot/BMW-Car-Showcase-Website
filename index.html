The M
Division

BMW M GmbH — formerly BMW Motorsport — was born to dominate the racetrack. What began as a racing programme evolved into a philosophy: that every road car bearing the M badge must be exceptional. Tested on the Nürburgring Nordschleife, refined through decades of competition, and built for those who demand more.

Today the M Division stands at the forefront of electrified performance, with the BMW XM marking a bold new era — proving that power and sustainability are no longer mutually exclusive.
